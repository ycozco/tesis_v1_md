Dataset strategy for the agroexport thesis

1. Primary source:
   - Anonymous operational data from the collaborating agroexport company.
   - Use for prediction, anomaly detection, SHAP explanations and report generation.

2. Public support data:
   - FAOSTAT: crop production and seasonality baseline.
   - UN Comtrade: export/trade context when needed.

3. Methodological benchmark:
   - ADBench: anomaly detection validation and robustness comparison.

4. Complementary strategy:
   - Synthetic anomalies injected into the operational dataset to test detector robustness.

Decision note:
   - The thesis should stay aligned to agroexport operations, not banking/fraud.
   - Public datasets are support and benchmarking assets; the operational dataset remains the main source.
